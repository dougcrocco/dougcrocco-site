Doug Crocco is an artist and curator based in Los Angeles, California. He has exhibited in New York, Los Angeles, Palm Beach, Miami, Boston, and Melbourne. He received his MFA from Claremont Graduate University in 2004. From 2015 to 2020 he was owner and director of the project space Big Pictures Los Angeles. He is currently producing new artworks.
